# InCam product conventions

Read these project-specific decisions in future InCam tasks. Preserve the existing app and the rollback checkpoint in `.checkpoints`.

- The user distinguishes image controls (color saturation, tint, HSL, WB, shutter, ISO) from on-screen composition/tools (ratio guides, grids, focus mode UI). Keep this distinction clear in layouts and explanations.
- Ratios and grids are composition overlays, not promises to crop or encode the recorded video. AF/MF is grouped with on-screen tools visually but does affect the actual lens focus.
- The camera occupies the left side at full screen height, ending at the floating dock's right edge, not centered on the phone. No shaded top/bottom margins; ratios are thin composition guides over the image. Reserve the remaining right side for thumb controls. Inspectors open on the right; only one inspector is open at a time.
- Keep one AF/MF toggle. MF opens a transparent right-thumb focus ruler matching the color controls; AF restores continuous autofocus.
- AF/MF sits in the camera rectangle's top-right corner. Compact WB/Shutter/ISO readouts stack VERTICALLY directly beneath it, right-aligned and preserving their small text size. The timer stays centered at the top. Their rulers' center values align exactly with Record's center, not with the readouts.
- The bottom dock is centered across the whole phone in landscape. The camera rectangle reaches its RIGHT edge. Its outer rounded outline is hidden, not deleted; retain the viewport and ratio/grid geometry. The right control area has a muted charcoal background distinct from the live image. Settings, Record, and ordinary adjustment dials share the SAME vertical column on the right; AE is the exception below.
- Camera Colors uses a thin HORIZONTAL color strip, 56 points wide to match the HUE/SAT/LUM list. Swipe left/right to select among six ranges. HUE/SAT/LUM remain a compact vertical selection list; sideways swipes enter/leave the selected dial and vertical ruler drags adjust values. Units are smaller than the numeric value. Colored adjustment dashes follow `Sources/Attachments/HSL-reference-2026-10-11.png`.
- Zoom plus/minus are slow controls: one tap changes 0.1×, holding repeats 0.1× once per second, releasing stops. Keep the existing separate camcorder drag behavior unless asked to change it.
- Double-tapping places a yellow target and opens a compact AE ruler beside it: right when it fits, otherwise left. The numeric value ALWAYS shares the target's vertical center; never clamp it independently. At screen edges, buttons extend inward rather than moving the number. Record stays available. Repeated double-taps reposition/reopen without unlocking. Plus/minus step ±0.3 EV; ruler steps are 0.1 EV. Dismissing keeps exposure locked; AE LOCK reopens it; the dial title unlocks.
- HSL currently changes recipe data, not live or recorded video rendering. Never claim otherwise without implementing and verifying that pipeline.
- Keep the HSL list compact: labels only, no list values or Adjust button. Show values only inside the active dial. Tap an axis to open it; sideways swipes enter/leave the dial. Keep the horizontal color selector thin with a larger invisible touch area.
- Camcorder drag zoom is always enabled in every zoom control; no on/off setting or direct-drag alternative. The right-hand charcoal control surface has rounded corners. AF and readouts share a trailing edge.
- Ratios settings uses green add/red remove controls over a curated list, not three numbered slots or an on/off switch. An empty list hides framing and stays empty after relaunch. Center guides in the unchanged viewport and dim unused areas with a partial black mask, including centered 1:1. This supersedes the earlier no-mask rule; saved video is not cropped.
- Audio sits below Grid in the left safe-area tool stack. Meters are vertical and use actual capture-channel levels. Input selection and gain use AVAudioSession; disable unsupported gain and input switching while recording. Simulator shows unavailable audio, never synthetic signals. EQ, compression and live playback are not implemented.
- Work in small reviewable increments, provide progress updates roughly every minute, preserve user changes, and document sources and limitations in `SOURCES.md`.
- Tint, Saturation, and Contrast display integer steps from -7 to +7, mapped to the existing normalized recipe strengths. Camera Colors is the visible name for the internal Color Mix page. Its numeric dial and neighbor previews use the same anchors as other color dials.
- WB/Shutter/ISO readouts below AF/MF show only values and A/M, without title/chevron lines. Keep accessible names. AE compensation is limited to -0.3...+0.3 EV, with 0.1 EV ruler steps.
- Shutter offers familiar speed presets and a muted Speed/Angle switch. Angle presets stop at 360 degrees and convert using current FPS; unsupported durations are excluded, retaining the manual 1/1500 limit.
- Latest Camera Colors interaction supersedes axis dial swapping: title at the same vertical anchor as Tint/Saturation/Contrast, right-aligned HUE/SAT/LUM labels, and an always-visible horizontal axis gradient below the separate color-range strip. Bottom navigation is ‹ CON and CC ›; CC returns through ‹ Camera Colors.
- CC means Custom Colors, with FIVE initially empty quick slots. Empty tap saves the draft; filled tap loads; another tap on the same loaded slot asks before overwriting with current settings. Cancel keeps the saved slot. Preserve the existing recipe library separately. These remain recipe-only, not live video effects.
- Audio meters replace the waveform icon below Grid: always show compact vertical tracks, tappable to open audio input settings. Unavailable input shows empty tracks, never fake levels; remove the separate duplicate meter beneath the button.
- HSL orientation correction: HUE/SAT/LUM are LEFT-aligned on the left; their always-visible adjustment gradient is VERTICAL on the right, up increases and down decreases. The separate color-range selector above stays horizontal. This supersedes the earlier horizontal axis-gradient/right-aligned-label instruction.
